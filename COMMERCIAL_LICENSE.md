# Commercial licensing

Commercial licensing is available for organizations that need permission to use DuckWebmail branding at larger scale or want commercial terms.

## Free tier

- Free use of DuckWebmail branding up to **100 mailboxes** (see `TRADEMARK.md`).

## Commercial use (over 100 mailboxes)

For deployments over **100 mailboxes**, contact:

- `commercial@mail-duck.com`

## Important

- This commercial license/trademark permission does **not** include the Stalwart mail server (or any other third-party software). Stalwart is licensed separately by its authors.
- DuckWebmail is provided **as is** unless a separate support agreement is signed.
